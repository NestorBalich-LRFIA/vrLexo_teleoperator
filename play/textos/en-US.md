Turn your phone into the remote control for the LexoDive robot.

With LexoDive VR Controller you drive the VR Lexo simulator robot from your phone and beat the challenges, or a physical robot connected over Bluetooth. Pick how to connect and go: the controls are the same.

TWO WAYS TO CONNECT
• Connect with QR: open vr.lexodive.com on the computer, turn on remote connection and scan the QR code that appears. Then you can name your robot.
• Connect via Bluetooth: turn on your physical robot, bring it close to the phone and pick it from the list. Next time, reconnect with one tap.

CONTROLS BUILT FOR PLAYING
• Big buttons to go forward, back and turn, with STOP in the middle.
• Drive by tilting the phone like a steering wheel.
• Fly by holding "Fly" or by moving the phone quickly up and down: the propeller follows how hard you move.
• Turn on the LED, activate the sword and sound the robot's buzzer.
• Altitude bar and level indicator to see how you are driving.
• Multi-touch: go forward, turn and fly at the same time.

FOR THE CLASSROOM
Each student drives their own robot from their phone, and the teacher sees all the robots in the simulator. If the connection drops, the app retries by itself.

You need the VR Lexo simulator (vr.lexodive.com) for QR mode, or a compatible robot for Bluetooth mode.
